Quick answers

Where can I get my food delivered?

To the Student Centre, Hostels, Lecture Halls or the Library. Pick one in your cart before you place the order.

How do I follow my order?

Open My orders in the menu bar. The status moves from Pending to Preparing to Ready.

How do I sell on FoodFever?

Sign up and choose Vendor. You will get a dashboard to manage incoming orders.

I made a mistake in my order

Send us a message with your order number as soon as you can, and we will pass it to the vendor.
